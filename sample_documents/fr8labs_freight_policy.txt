FR8LABS GLOBAL FREIGHT FORWARDING
STANDARD OPERATING POLICY & CUSTOMER GUIDELINES
Document Ref: F8-POL-2025-001 | Version 3.2 | Effective: 1 January 2025
Classification: Internal / Customer-Facing Summary

================================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

This policy defines standard terms, transit commitments, documentation
requirements, and claims procedures for ocean, air, and multimodal shipments
handled by Fr8Labs and its appointed agents. It applies to all quotations (QT),
booking confirmations, and house bills of lading (HBL) issued under Fr8Labs
reference numbers unless superseded by a signed customer master service agreement.

================================================================================
2. QUOTATION VALIDITY AND RATE APPLICATION
================================================================================

2.1  All spot quotations remain valid for fourteen (14) calendar days from the
     date of issue unless otherwise stated on the QT.

2.2  Quoted rates assume general cargo, factory stuffing, and standard free time
     at origin and destination as noted on the quotation. Deviations require
     re-confirmation before booking.

2.3  Rate adjustments may apply if cargo weight or volume varies by more than
     ten percent (10%) from the quoted figures, or if the shipper changes the
     port pair after booking acceptance.

2.4  Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF), Currency Adjustment Factor (CAF), and
     Peak Season Surcharge (PSS) are pass-through charges and may be applied
     at time of shipment even if not itemised on the original QT.

================================================================================

================================================================================
4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

4.1  Commercial Invoice — must show shipper, consignee, HS code, Incoterm,
     unit value, and country of origin. Minimum three (3) copies for ocean;
     two (2) for air.

4.2  Packing List — required for all shipments. Must match invoice quantities
     and gross/net weights within 2% tolerance.

4.3  Certificate of Origin — mandatory for preferential duty claims under
     ASEAN-India FTA, EU GSP, or US GSP where applicable.

4.4  Fumigation Certificate — required for all wooden packaging (pallets,
     crates, dunnage) on Australia and New Zealand lanes.

4.5  Dangerous Goods Declaration (DGD) — IMDG-compliant for ocean; IATA DGR
     for air. Booking must be submitted minimum seventy-two (72) hours before
     cut-off for DG cargo.

4.6  Letter of Credit (LC) shipments — draft BL and documents must be
     presented for approval minimum five (5) business days before vessel ETD.

================================================================================
5. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE
================================================================================

5.1  Fr8Labs liability as freight forwarder is limited to USD 2.00 per
     kilogram or the value of the goods, whichever is lower, unless a higher
     limit is agreed in writing prior to shipment.

5.2  Carrier liability applies per applicable convention (Hague-Visby, Montreal
     Convention, or CMNI) depending on mode and lane.

5.3  Comprehensive cargo insurance is strongly recommended. Fr8Labs can arrange
     all-risk coverage at 0.35% of invoice value plus freight, minimum premium
     USD 50 per shipment.

5.4  Claims for visible damage must be noted on the delivery receipt at time of
     delivery. Concealed damage claims must be filed within three (3) business
     days of delivery.

================================================================================
6. FREE TIME AND DETENTION / DEMURRAGE
================================================================================

6.1  Standard import free time at destination port:
     - FCL: 7 days combined (detention + demurrage) unless carrier tariff
       states otherwise.
     - LCL: 5 days at CFS; storage charges apply thereafter at USD 18/CBM/day.

6.2  Export free time at origin: 5 days from empty container release. Day 6
     onward: USD 45 per 20' container / USD 75 per 40' container per day.

6.3  Fr8Labs will notify the consignee at day 3 of free time remaining via
     email to the contact on the booking.

================================================================================
7. PROHIBITED AND RESTRICTED CARGO
================================================================================

The following are not accepted without prior written approval from Fr8Labs
Compliance:

  - Explosives, ammunition, and military hardware
  - Lithium batteries (standalone, UN3480/3481) above IATA Section II limits
  - Live animals and perishable goods requiring temperature control (reefer)
  - Counterfeit goods, sanctioned-party cargo, and dual-use items under
    EAR/ITAR without export licence
  - Personal effects exceeding USD 5,000 declared value on commercial lanes

================================================================================
8. PAYMENT TERMS
================================================================================

8.1  Standard credit customers: invoices due net thirty (30) days from invoice
     date. New customers: prepayment or CAD basis until credit limit approved.

8.2  Disbursement invoices (duty, port charges, trucking) are due within seven
     (7) days of presentation regardless of freight payment terms.

8.3  A late payment fee of 1.5% per month applies to overdue balances.

================================================================================
9. CLAIMS PROCEDURE
================================================================================

Step 1: Notify Fr8Labs claims desk at claims@fr8labs.co within seven (7) days
        of delivery (or expected delivery for total loss).

Step 2: Submit claim form F8-CLM-01 with supporting documents: commercial
        invoice, packing list, photos, survey report (if applicable), and
        original damaged cargo retention proof.

Step 3: Fr8Labs will acknowledge within two (2) business days and issue a claim
        reference number (format: CLM-YYYY-NNNNN).

Step 4: Settlement target: forty-five (45) days from complete documentation
        receipt, subject to carrier/insurer response times.

Maximum claim filing deadline: nine (9) months from date of delivery.
